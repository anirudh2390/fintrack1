# FinTrack

A comprehensive financial tracking and management application designed to help users monitor their income, expenses, budgets, and financial goals with ease.

---

## 🚀 Features

* **Expense & Income Tracking:** Easily record, categorize, and manage daily transactions.
* **Budget Management:** Set monthly or category-specific spending limits to stay on track.
* **Interactive Dashboard:** Get a clear overview of your financial health through summaries and visualizations.
* **Secure Data Storage:** Keep your financial records organized and secure.
